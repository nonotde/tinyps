Edit GIF Frame by Frame, Without Uploading

An animated GIF is just a stack of frames. TinyPS splits the GIF into separate editable pages, lets you change each one, and writes everything back as a real animated GIF — all locally in your browser.

A GIF frame editor lets you open an animation, work on individual frames, and export the result as an animated GIF. TinyPS does this inside a browser extension: every frame becomes a page you can crop, rotate, watermark or compress. Up to 60 frames, no upload, no sign-up.

How to edit GIF frames in TinyPS

  1. Install the TinyPS extension and open the editor.
  2. Drag in an animated GIF. TinyPS decodes it and puts each frame on its own page, keeping the original frame delay.
  3. Edit any page — crop, resize, rotate, add text or a watermark, apply a filter, or remove a background.
  4. Click Export animated GIF. TinyPS renders every page through the full editing pipeline and writes a real animated GIF back to your device.

What makes this different from a web GIF editor

Most GIF frame editors are websites: you upload the file, edit in a tab, and download. TinyPS is a browser extension, so the GIF editor lives alongside your normal photo tools in one multi-page workspace.

Common GIF frame edits

  • Delete a slow opening or a messy final frame by removing that page.
  • Crop or resize every frame at once so the whole animation fits a new size.
  • Rotate a shaky clip a fraction of a degree with the 1° fine nudge.
  • Add a text caption or watermark to selected frames as a layer.
  • Compress a heavy GIF by re-encoding frames before export.

Why local GIF editing matters

GIFs often contain screenshots, private moments or unreleased work. With TinyPS the frames never leave your device and there is no account to sign up for. Processing uses your browser's own decoder and a local encoder, so the editor also works with no internet connection once installed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I edit a GIF frame by frame?

Open the GIF in TinyPS. Each frame becomes a separate page in the multi-page workspace. Edit the pages you want, then choose Export animated GIF to write them back into one animation.

Does the GIF editor upload my animation?

No. Decoding, editing and GIF encoding all happen in your browser on your device. There is no server round-trip and no account required.

How many frames can TinyPS handle?

Up to 60 frames per animation. Longer GIFs are truncated to the first 60 frames with a clear notice, so the editor stays responsive.

Can I delete or reorder GIF frames?

Yes. Because every frame is a page, you can remove a frame by deleting that page and rearrange frames by reordering pages in the thumbnail strip.

Can I add text or a watermark to a GIF?

Yes. Text and fill layers work on each frame just like on a still image. Add a caption or watermark as a layer and keep it editable until export.

Does the export keep my edits and frame timing?

Yes. TinyPS renders each edited page through its normal pipeline and encodes the result with a real LZW GIF encoder, preserving the animation and frame delays.

Can I use the GIF editor offline?

Yes. TinyPS runs locally and keeps working without an internet connection after the extension is installed.
