Remove Image Background Without Uploading a Single Pixel

Removing an image background usually means handing your photo to somebody's server. TinyPS lets you remove image background without uploading a single pixel — an on-device AI model does the work.

Yes — with TinyPS you remove image background without uploading anything. TinyPS runs the segmentation model (u2netp) inside your browser through WebAssembly, so the file is read from your disk, processed on your device, and written back to your disk with no network request at all. Background removal is a Pro feature; the free tier covers crop, resize, compression, conversion and layers.

How to remove a background offline

It takes about 20 seconds and zero uploads:
  1. Install the TinyPS extension for Chrome or Edge (or open the free online editor — no install).
  2. Open your image inside TinyPS.
  3. Click “Remove background”. The u2netp model runs locally via WebAssembly.
  4. Export a PNG with transparency, or drop it onto another layer to keep editing.

Why “no upload” actually matters

When a tool uploads your photo, it stores it, may train on it, and becomes a target for breaches. With TinyPS the file stays on your device:
  • No cloud account, no API key, no queue.
  • Confidential screenshots, IDs and product shots never touch a server.
  • EXIF location and metadata are not leaked to a third party.
  • Works on a plane, in a basement, anywhere — fully offline.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove a background from a photo without uploading it?

Yes. TinyPS removes backgrounds entirely on your device — the AI model is bundled with the extension and executed locally via ONNX Runtime WebAssembly. Your photo is never sent to a server, which is why it also works with the network disconnected.

How do I remove the background of an image offline?

Install TinyPS from the Chrome Web Store (or open the free online editor), load the image, switch to the Pro tools, and pick Remove background. Export as PNG or WebP with an alpha channel. No account and no internet connection are required.

Is there a free way to remove an image background?

The core editor is free forever — cropping, resizing, compression, format conversion, text and layers. AI background removal is a Pro feature, available as a one-time $24.90 purchase, $1.99/month or $9.90/year, and one licence covers up to three devices.

Will removing the background reduce my image quality?

No. TinyPS keeps the original resolution and only writes an alpha channel into the output, so the visible pixels are untouched. Export to PNG or WebP for transparency, or to JPEG with a solid or mosaic fill if you need a flat background.

Which format should I export a transparent image as?

PNG is the safest universal choice — lossless transparency supported everywhere. WebP gives smaller files with the same transparency and is supported by all current browsers. Avoid JPEG for cut-outs: it has no alpha channel, so transparent areas turn black or white.

Does background removal work on hair and fine edges?

Salient-object segmentation handles people and common objects well, including most hair and edges, but very fine strands against a busy background can be imperfect. If a few edges need cleanup, crop slightly tighter or mask the area by hand before exporting.
