How to Activate TinyPS Pro

← TinyPS home · Purchase happens inside the extension (✨ Pro button in the top bar).

1
Buy Pro
Open the TinyPS editor, click the ✨ Pro button in the top bar and pick a plan (Lifetime / Yearly / Monthly). Checkout opens in a new tab.
2
Check your email
After payment you receive a receipt email containing your license key (looks like XXXXXXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXXXXXXXXX).
3
Paste & activate
Back in the editor, click ✨ Pro again, paste the key into the license field and click Activate. Pro features unlock immediately.
4
Re-login anywhere
Your license works on up to 3 devices. Switching computers? Use “Deactivate this device” in the Pro dialog to free a slot, then activate on the new machine.

FAQ

Which payment methods are available?

Lifetime (one-time): Alipay, WeChat Pay, cards. Subscriptions (monthly/yearly): cards & PayPal. This is a payment-processor limitation — wallets don't support recurring billing.

Do I need to be online?

Only for activation and an occasional background re-check. Once activated, Pro keeps working offline for 14 days at a time.

What happens when a subscription expires?

Pro features lock again, but nothing you made is lost. Lifetime keys never expire.

Lost your key or receipt?

Email nonotde@qq.com with your payment email and we'll resend it.

如何激活 TinyPS Pro

1
购买：打开 TinyPS 编辑器，点顶栏 ✨ Pro 按钮选套餐（终身 / 年付 / 月付），新标签页完成付款。
2
收邮件：付款后收据邮件里包含你的许可证密钥（形如 XXXXXXXX-XXXX-…）。
3
粘贴激活：回到编辑器再点 ✨ Pro，把密钥粘贴进输入框点激活，Pro 功能立即解锁。
4
多设备：一个密钥最多 3 台设备。换电脑时先在 Pro 弹窗点“停用此设备”释放名额，再到新设备激活。

常见问题：终身档支持支付宝/微信；订阅档仅支持卡和 PayPal（支付通道限制，钱包不支持自动续费）。激活后可离线使用，每 14 天后台自动续验一次。订阅到期 Pro 功能重新锁定，但已制作的内容不会丢失。丢失密钥请发邮件到 nonotde@qq.com（附付款邮箱）。
