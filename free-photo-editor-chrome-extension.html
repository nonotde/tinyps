Free Photo Editor Chrome Extension — Local, No Watermark

Most free online photo editors add a watermark or upload your photo to justify it. TinyPS is a free photo editor Chrome extension that does neither — everything runs locally on your machine.

A free photo editor Chrome extension that adds no watermark and uploads nothing: TinyPS runs entirely on your device. Crop, resize, rotate, add text and filters, convert formats, remove backgrounds and export — free, with no account, no credits and no watermark on your output.

What the free extension does

  • Crop, rotate, resize with preset ratios.
  • Layers, text, filters and canvas tools.
  • Compress and convert JPEG/PNG/WebP/BMP/GIF.
  • Multi-page batch and ZIP export.
  • No watermark on any export.

Install in seconds

  1. Open the Chrome Web Store page for TinyPS.
  2. Click Add to Chrome.
  3. Right-click any web image and choose “Edit image with TinyPS”, or open the extension.
  4. Start editing — no sign-up.

Why a local extension beats a tab tool

A local extension works offline, adds a right-click menu, and never uploads your image. Tab-only tools often upload files and show watermarks on free exports.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free photo editor for Chrome?

Yes. TinyPS is free for Chrome and Edge: cropping, resizing, rotation, layers, text, filters, compression, format conversion and batch ZIP export all work at no cost. Pro adds free-form cropping, fill layers and AI background removal.

Does the Chrome photo editor add a watermark?

No. TinyPS never stamps anything onto your image. Exports contain only what you edited — the watermark-free output is the same whether you use the free tier or Pro.

Which free Chrome image editor doesn't upload my files?

Any editor that processes the file inside the browser qualifies. TinyPS is one of them: the image is read from your disk, processed locally and exported locally, so it works with the network switched off.

How do I edit a photo in Chrome without an account?

Install TinyPS and start editing — there is no sign-up step and no profile. You can also use the free in-browser editor without installing anything at all.

Is there a Chrome extension to edit images offline?

Yes. TinyPS works fully offline once installed: the editor, the encoders and the background-removal model are all local, so editing keeps working with no internet connection.

Is a Chrome extension photo editor better than a web app?

For privacy and speed, usually: an extension processes files on your machine without uploading them, opens straight from a right-click on any image on the web, and works offline. A web app is more convenient when you cannot install anything, which is why TinyPS offers both.
