TinyPS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20 · ← TinyPS home

Short version

Your images never leave your device. All editing, compression, AI background removal and format conversion run entirely inside your browser. TinyPS has no servers, no analytics and no trackers.

What stays on your device

  • Images: loaded, edited and exported 100% locally. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Settings: theme, language and other preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage. They never leave your device.

What TinyPS sends out — and why

TinyPS offers optional paid "Pro" features (free crop, fill layers, AI background removal). To use them you enter a license key purchased via our reseller Creem. When you activate or validate a license, TinyPS sends:

  • Your license key — to check that it is valid.
  • A random device identifier (e.g. tinyps-k3x9p2qa) — generated locally, not derived from any personal information, used to count device activations.

These two values are sent only to Creem's license-validation API. No images, browsing history or other data are ever transmitted. If you never buy Pro, TinyPS makes no network requests at all (the optional "load image from URL" feature fetches only the image address you choose).

Payments

Payments are processed by Creem, our merchant of record. Your payment details (email, payment method) are handled by Creem and are subject to the Creem Privacy Policy. We never see or store your card number or wallet credentials.

Deactivation & removal

You can deactivate a device from the Pro dialog at any time, which deletes the license data stored in your browser. Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data.

Questions about this policy: nonotde@qq.com

TinyPS 隐私政策

更新日期：2026-10-20

简短说明

你的图片永远不会离开设备。所有编辑、压缩、AI 去除背景和格式转换全部在你的浏览器内完成。TinyPS 没有服务器、没有统计、没有追踪器。

保存在你设备上的内容

  • 图片：载入、编辑、导出全程本地进行，不上传任何内容。
  • 设置：主题、语言等偏好保存在浏览器 localStorage 中，不会离开设备。

TinyPS 会发送什么、为什么

TinyPS 提供可选的付费 Pro 功能（自由裁剪、填充图层、AI 去除背景）。购买后需输入许可证密钥激活。激活或校验时，TinyPS 仅向经销商 Creem 的许可证校验接口发送：

  • 许可证密钥——用于验证有效性；
  • 随机设备标识（如 tinyps-k3x9p2qa）——本地随机生成，不含任何个人信息，仅用于统计激活设备数。

不会传输图片、浏览记录或任何其他数据。未购买 Pro 时，除"从网址载入图片"会请求你指定的图片地址外，扩展不做任何网络请求。

支付

付款由 Creem（经销商）处理，邮箱与支付信息受其隐私政策约束，我们无法看到你的卡号或钱包凭据。

停用与删除

你可在 Pro 弹窗中随时停用设备，这将删除浏览器中保存的许可证数据；卸载扩展会删除全部本地数据。
