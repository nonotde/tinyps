Quick answers

How do I activate Pro after buying?

See the step-by-step activation guide — your license key arrives by email right after purchase.

Which payment methods are available?

Lifetime (one-time): Alipay and cards. Monthly/yearly subscriptions: cards. Wallets can't be used for auto-renewing subscriptions.

How many devices can I use?

One license works on up to 3 devices. Switching machines? "Deactivate this device" in the Pro dialog frees a slot.

Lost my license key / receipt

Email us from your purchase address and we'll resend it.

Refunds

If your key doesn't work or you purchased by accident, contact us within 14 days for a full refund — see Terms §4.

My images never leave my device?

Correct — all editing, compression and AI background removal run 100% locally. See the Privacy Policy.

常见问题

购买后如何激活 Pro？

见激活指引——付款后密钥会立即发送到邮箱。

支持哪些支付方式？

终身买断（一次性）：支付宝与信用卡；月付/年付订阅：信用卡（钱包类支付不支持自动续费）。

能在几台设备上使用？

一个密钥最多 3 台设备。换电脑时在 Pro 弹窗点"停用此设备"即可释放名额。

丢失密钥/收据

用购买邮箱联系我们，我们会重新发送。

退款

密钥无法激活或误购，14 天内联系可全额退款——见条款第 4 节。

图片真的不出设备吗？

是的——编辑、压缩、AI 去背景全部本地运行，详见隐私政策。
