## 描述段落（长，用于需要详情的站点）

TinyPS is a free, lightweight image and photo editor that runs entirely in your browser as a Chrome extension — think of it as a simplified, fully offline Photoshop. Every crop, resize, compress, format conversion, and AI background removal happens 100% locally on your device. Your photos never leave your computer. No server, no account, no tracking. It supports multi-layer compositing, batch export to ZIP, and AI background removal powered by u2netp + ONNX Runtime WebAssembly — all running on your CPU, with no cloud, no API calls, no upload.
