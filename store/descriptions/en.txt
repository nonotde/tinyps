TinyPS is a free, lightweight image editor and photo editor Chrome extension — think of it as a simplified, fully offline Photoshop that works right in your browser. Every crop, resize, rotation, compression, format conversion, watermarking, and AI background removal is done 100% locally on your device. Your photos never leave your computer. No servers, no accounts, no tracking.

Why You Need an Offline Image Editor?
Most tools that bill themselves as "online photo editors" upload your images to someone else's servers first, where they get processed, indexed, and often stored for an unpredictable length of time — sometimes even used to train AI models. TinyPS does the exact opposite. It's a browser-based Photoshop alternative that runs entirely on your own machine. Private and sensitive photos stay private. If you've ever searched for "online Photoshop" but hesitated at the upload button, TinyPS is the answer. You get the power of desktop software without handing over a single file.

At a time when data privacy matters more than ever, local processing isn't just convenient — it's a form of protection. TinyPS never sends any personal data to third parties and never stores your files on someone else's servers. Whether it's the "minimum necessary" principle required by the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL) or simply your own peace of mind, local processing is compliant by design.

Create with Real Layers
TinyPS isn't just a single-image touch-up tool — it's a true multi-layer editor:
- Paste any image in as a new layer, add text layers, and freely reorder, lock, hide, and merge them
- Transform each layer independently: scale, move, rotate 90/180/270 degrees, flip, reset — with a 10-step undo history
- Use crop presets (1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 3:2, 9:16) for free; free crop is included in Pro and adds Keep Inside / Keep Outside: Keep Inside leaves only the selected area, while Keep Outside does the reverse, deleting what's inside the box and keeping the surroundings. Full horizontal or vertical bands are stitched back together automatically, and holes cut out locally reveal a transparent background
- Free Rotate (Pro): drag the icon at the center of the canvas to rotate the image around its center to any angle; a 90° magnetic snap automatically aligns it as you approach 90°, so straightening a crooked photo takes just a few seconds
- Watermark text opacity is fully adjustable (100% by default); newly added text automatically inherits the previous text's rotation angle, so you can lay out an entire sheet of diagonal watermarks without repositioning each one
- Work with live thumbnails of every layer and move elements precisely with your mouse or keyboard

Layers turn TinyPS from a simple touch-up tool into something capable of full compositions: business cards, social media graphics, product collages, or simply cutting a subject out cleanly and placing it on a new background. You can overlay a logo as its own layer on a photo and adjust its position and transparency anytime, without ever worrying about damaging the original.

Compression and Conversion Without Losing Quality
- Lossless pass-through: files you haven't edited are copied byte for byte, so there's no quality loss from re-encoding
- Hit an exact target file size, strip metadata (EXIF), and fine-tune quality with a single slider
- Convert between JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and GIF, now with AVIF export
- Batch-export every page into a single ZIP, with no external tools required
- The new AVIF export runs on a local WASM encoder, lazy-loaded, no impact on startup — it's only loaded the first time you use it, so it never slows the extension down

For site owners, online shop sellers, and content creators, this is a genuine lifesaver: open a product photo and TinyPS takes it from 5 MB down to 300 KB with no visible difference, so your pages load faster. An exact target size means you define the trade-off between file size and sharpness, instead of blindly "lowering quality." And when you need something even smaller, you can export straight to AVIF.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does TinyPS upload my images? No. Editing makes zero network requests. The only outbound traffic occurs when you load an image from a URL or activate a Pro license (a license key plus a random device ID, sent through our proxy to the payment provider). Images are never transmitted, stored, or analyzed.
Can I use it offline? Yes. Once installed, cropping, compression, conversion, and background removal all work without a network connection.
Can it replace Photoshop for quick tasks? For cropping, resizing, compression, conversion, adding text, and cutting out subjects, yes. It isn't a full raster editor: there are no brushes, no masks, and no CMYK.
Does it support WebP and AVIF? WebP can be both read and written. AVIF export is now supported: encoding is handled by a local WASM encoder, lazy-loaded, no impact on startup; for now, you can't open an AVIF file directly as a source image to edit.
Will the new AVIF export slow the extension down? No. The AVIF encoder is loaded on demand only the first time you use it, takes up no startup time otherwise, and never connects to the network.
What do Keep Inside / Keep Outside mean in free crop? Keep Inside leaves only the selected area; Keep Outside does the reverse, deleting what's inside the box and keeping the surroundings. Full horizontal or vertical bands are stitched back together automatically, and holes cut out locally reveal a transparent background, which makes cut-outs and wiping away a patch of the image effortless.
Can Free Rotate turn to any angle? Yes. In Pro, drag the icon at the center of the canvas to rotate the image around its center to any angle, with a 90° magnetic snap that kicks in as you approach 90°, so straightening a crooked photo is quick. It doesn't conflict with the free version's built-in 90/180/270-degree right-angle rotation: the former is continuous fine-tuning, the latter precise right angles.
Can I adjust watermark text opacity? Yes, it's 100% opaque by default and can be dialed down as needed. On top of that, newly added text automatically inherits the previous text's rotation angle, so a whole sheet of diagonal watermarks doesn't need to be repositioned one by one.
Is there a batch mode? Yes. Open multiple images, adjust each page, then export them all into a single ZIP.
Is Pro a subscription? You can buy a lifetime license, or choose monthly / annual plans. A single license covers up to three devices.
Is my license secure? Activation goes through a proxy and sends only the license key and an anonymous device ID. No image data is ever transmitted.
Does it work on Mac/Windows/Linux? Yes. TinyPS supports all common desktop operating systems on Chrome and Chromium-based browsers.
Is there a Chinese interface? Yes, both Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese are among the 10 built-in interface languages.
Is the free version enough? For the vast majority of everyday tasks — compression, conversion, cropping, adding text, collages — it's more than enough. You only need Pro if you want AI background removal, free crop, or Free Rotate.
Will it slow down? Processing happens locally, so speed depends on your computer; compression and conversion of typical photos are instant, and background removal usually finishes within a few seconds.
