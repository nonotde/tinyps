TinyPS — Privacy-First Local Image Editor (Chrome Extension)

TinyPS is a fully local-running image editing extension built for privacy-conscious users. Your photos never leave your device: all processing happens in your browser, on your machine. No uploads, no servers, no accounts, no telemetry.

Key Features
• Edit any web image in one click — right-click any image on a web page and choose "Edit image with TinyPS" to open it instantly in the local editor.
• AI background removal — a lightweight on-device model removes backgrounds locally, no network needed.
• Batch editing — batch crop, resize, apply filters, and convert formats.
• Local compression — shrink file size dramatically, all on your machine, nothing uploaded.
• Full toolkit — crop, rotate, filters, adjustments, watermark, and format conversion (JPG / PNG / WEBP / BMP / GIF and more).

What's New
1. Free crop with Keep-Inside / Keep-Outside — Keep-Outside deletes the area inside the box. Full-width or full-height strips are auto-stitched, and local holes are punched through to transparent, so you can carve out shapes or clear specific regions.
2. Free Rotate (Pro) — drag the center handle to rotate the image around its center at any angle, with 90° magnetic snap for quick, precise turns.
3. Watermark text opacity — set text transparency (default 100%). New text now inherits the previous text's rotation state, making multi-line angled watermarks much faster to place.
4. AVIF export — a new output format encoded locally via WASM. It is lazy-loaded, so it never slows down the app's startup.

How to Use
• Web images: right-click any image on a page → "Edit image with TinyPS".
• Local files: open the extension and pick a local image.
• Online editor: the website's Online Editor also runs entirely locally in your browser.

Privacy Promise
All computation runs on your device. We do not collect, upload, or store any of your images or edits. No accounts, no telemetry, no third-party data selling.

Supported formats: import JPG / PNG / WEBP / BMP / GIF; export JPG / PNG / WEBP / BMP / GIF / AVIF.
