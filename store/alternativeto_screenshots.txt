# TinyPS — AlternativeTo 截图说明（英文，逐项粘贴到对应截图）

---

## 01-offline.png
TinyPS runs fully offline as a Chrome extension — no upload, no account, no tracking. Your photos never leave your computer.

## 02-crop.png
Crop to preset ratios for free and watch the file size drop instantly; the status bar shows the new size and the savings in real time.

## 03-background.png
Remove image backgrounds locally with on-device AI — the model runs in WebAssembly, so nothing is ever sent to a server.

## 04-export.png
Compress to an exact file size and convert between JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP and GIF, then export every page as one ZIP.

## 05-pro.png
Core crop and export are free; Pro adds freeform crop, fill layers, an eyedropper and one-click AI background removal.
